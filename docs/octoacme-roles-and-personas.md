# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers conduct user research, create wireframes and prototypes, and validate usability throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure that user needs and best practices in interaction design are reflected in the final product.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validate assumptions with stakeholders
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Lead usability testing and gather feedback
- Collaborate with developers on design feasibility and handoff
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design principles
- Document design decisions and maintain design systems

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered solutions that are intuitive and accessible
- Reduce rework through early validation and feedback
- Establish consistent design patterns across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with Product Managers and Developers
- Usability testing reports and insights
- Collaboration during sprint planning and retrospectives
- Design documentation and component libraries

### Interacts with
- **Product Managers**: Align on user needs and product strategy
- **Developers**: Provide design specs, review implementation, iterate on feedback
- **QA/Testing**: Collaborate on usability test scenarios and acceptance criteria

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and the delivery team. They elicit requirements, document scope, clarify priorities, and translate business needs into actionable work items for the development team.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document requirements from stakeholders
- Create detailed business specifications and use cases
- Translate business needs into clear, prioritized backlog items
- Conduct impact analysis for scope changes and trade-offs
- Validate that solutions meet business requirements
- Support test planning and acceptance criteria definition

### Goals
- Ensure clarity and alignment between business goals and technical delivery
- Minimize scope creep and rework through thorough requirements gathering
- Enable faster decision-making through clear documentation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Detailed specification documents and change logs
- Weekly syncs with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Acceptance criteria refinement with Developers and QA

### Interacts with
- **Product Managers**: Clarify product vision and validate requirements
- **Project Managers**: Support scope management and timeline planning
- **Developers**: Answer clarifying questions and validate technical feasibility
- **Stakeholders**: Conduct interviews and communicate findings

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach the team on agile best practices. They enable a high-performing, self-operating team and maintain velocity and team morale.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove impediments to team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Track sprint progress and maintain burndown charts
- Support continuous improvement initiatives
- Escalate blockers when necessary to Project Managers and leadership

### Goals
- Maintain predictable sprint velocity and delivery cadence
- Foster a culture of collaboration, transparency, and psychological safety
- Continuously improve team processes and efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members to address concerns
- Blocker escalation and resolution tracking
- Retrospective follow-ups and action item tracking

### Interacts with
- **All delivery roles**: Facilitate collaboration and remove barriers
- **Project Managers**: Escalate risks and resource constraints
- **Product Managers**: Support backlog refinement and sprint planning

---

## Customer Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support Leads represent the voice of the customer throughout the project lifecycle. They relay post-deployment feedback, identify pain points, and assist in defining incident response and support procedures to ensure customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and document common issues or feature requests
- Participate in requirements gathering to represent customer needs
- Define support runbooks and incident response procedures
- Assist with release planning and customer communication
- Validate that solutions address real customer pain points
- Support deployment and post-release monitoring

### Goals
- Ensure customer perspectives inform product decisions
- Reduce support burden through proactive issue identification
- Improve customer satisfaction and reduce incident response time

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend analysis
- Release notes and customer communication drafts
- Incident playbooks and support documentation
- Weekly syncs with Product Managers and Developers on critical bugs

### Interacts with
- **Product Managers**: Prioritize customer-facing features and improvements
- **Developers**: Report production issues and collaborate on hotfixes
- **Project Managers**: Inform release communications and support readiness
- **QA/Testing**: Validate acceptance criteria from a customer perspective

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When assigning work, clarify which roles are involved and ensure clear handoffs between functions.
